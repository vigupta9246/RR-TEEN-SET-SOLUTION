Common Shed Problems We Solve in Noida

Warehouse roofs not sized for actual load: A common issue we find in older Phase 1 & 2 units is roofing that was sized for a previous, lighter use of the space. When a business changes what it stores or manufactures, the existing shed frame sometimes isn't rated for the new load — we assess this during the site visit rather than assuming the existing structure is adequate.

Fire-NOC-related roofing gaps: Noida Authority and fire department clearances increasingly expect proper ventilation, fire-resistant sheeting choices, and clear exit-path coverage in factory sheds. We design with these requirements in mind so clients aren't caught out during an inspection.

Terrace seepage in Noida Extension flats: Similar to other NCR residential belts, flat RCC terraces in Greater Noida West societies see monsoon seepage. A properly sloped rooftop shed resolves this more permanently than repeated waterproofing.

Parking shortfall in older sectors: Housing societies in the earlier-developed sectors (Sector 15-50 range) were often built with less parking than current vehicle ownership demands. We install bolted multi-bay car parking sheds without major civil work.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Noida

For Sector 63/64 and other industrial-plot units, load requirement is the single biggest cost driver — a warehouse storing finished garments needs a lighter frame than one holding heavy electronics assembly equipment, and pricing follows the steel section that job actually needs. Clear span (how far apart support columns can be, important for factory floor movement and machinery layout) is the second major factor. For residential work in Noida Extension and Greater Noida West, terrace access and whether a shed needs to integrate with existing building services (drainage outlets, water tanks) affect labour time and final cost. We confirm exact numbers only after an in-person survey.

Materials We Use for Noida Projects

For Sector 63/64 factory and warehouse sheds, we use IS-grade steel sections with galvanized or colour-coated sheeting, chosen based on the specific load and fire-resistance requirements of the unit — export and electronics units in particular often need documentation on the sheeting's fire rating for their compliance files, which we provide. For residential rooftop work, we offer both standard profile sheeting and PUF sandwich panels for clients wanting better heat insulation on the top floor.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your Sector 63/64 warehouse shed has surface rust but the frame remains structurally sound and rated for your current load, a re-sheet is usually the more economical option. If you've changed what the unit stores or manufactures and the existing frame wasn't designed for the new load, or if fire-NOC requirements have flagged the roofing as non-compliant, a redesigned or new structure is the safer route. We offer a free inspection to confirm which situation applies before recommending anything.
