Who we are

A fabrication and installation contractor, supervised from survey to handover

R.R Teen Set Solution is a GST-registered tin shed (teen shed), steel structure, PEB and fabrication contractor based in Najafgarh, New Delhi. We were established in 2016 with one aim: to give Delhi NCR businesses well-built steel sheds and structures at honest prices.

We build for homes, shops, factories and warehouses, and we take projects from about 100 sq.ft to more than 1,00,000 sq.ft. We are a contractor: we fabricate and install. We are not a steel manufacturer or a franchise, which is why the person who surveys your site is the same team that builds it.

Our team of fabricators, engineers and site workers works across Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad, and takes projects across the wider NCR. More than 500 projects are complete, and our IndiaMART profile carries a 5.0 rating as a verified supplier.

What we build

Sheds and steel structures, fabricated and installed by our own team

Tin shed fabrication

Residential, commercial and industrial tin sheds with IS-grade GI sheets: terrace sheds, car parking sheds and shop-front sheds. Tin shed details

Industrial shed

Factory and manufacturing sheds with an MS frame and earthquake-resistant design, up to 60 m clear span and EOT crane support from 2 to 10 ton. Industrial sheds

PEB structure

Pre-engineered buildings up to 90 m span, designed to IS 800 and IS 1893 (seismic). Faster to build than RCC, with span, height, slope and colour customised. PEB structures

Prefab warehouse

Weather-proof warehouses for logistics, storage and cold storage. Forklift-compatible, clear height 7 to 12 m, with loading bays and wide gates. Prefab warehouses

Roofing, repair and waterproofing

Terrace, commercial and industrial roofing, plus repair of leaking, rusted or storm-damaged roofs and monsoon waterproofing. Roofing

Agricultural and other structures

Sheds for poultry, dairy and crop storage, prefab factory sheds and buildings, and MS fabrication such as trusses, columns and purlins. Agricultural sheds

Commercial terms

Billing, payment, warranty and support, stated up front

GST and invoicing

A proper GST invoice on every project, useful for B2B and government work. Quoted rates are before GST. GST is 18% for commercial and industrial sheds; some agricultural structures may qualify for a lower rate, confirmed at the site visit.

Payment terms

Typically 50% advance and 50% on completion. Large projects can be milestone-based: booking advance, payment at material delivery and balance on completion. We accept bank transfer, UPI, NEFT and cheque.

Warranty

Standard workmanship warranty on fabrication and installation. Where applicable, the manufacturer warranty on sheeting is passed through to you. Terms are confirmed for each project.

After-sales support

Raise a ticket on our Support page for quality, delay, billing or GST, and warranty issues. Billing queries are usually resolved in 24 to 48 hours and site-visit issues in 3 to 7 days.

Permissions and documents

Most residential and small commercial tin sheds inside your own plot need no separate permission. Large industrial sheds, or rooftop terrace sheds in Delhi, may fall under DDA or MCD norms, and our team guides you during planning.

Keep these ready

For an industrial shed, keep the plot layout (naksha) and land documents ready. Approvals are discussed at the site visit. Prices depend on size, span, height, material and location, so a written quotation follows the visit.
