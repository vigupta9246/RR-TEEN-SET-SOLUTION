Welder — Steel Shed & PEB Fabrication

Full-time role at R.R Teen Set Solution, Najafgarh, New Delhi. Apply by 31 Dec 2026.

Freshers (ITI) + Experienced

About the role

Arc/MIG welding of MS columns, trusses, purlins & site joints for tin sheds, industrial sheds and PEB structures.

Day to day

  • Structural joint welding as per site drawing
  • Checking weld quality & finish before handover
  • Working alongside the fitting team during erection

Experience needed

2+ yrs arc/MIG welding preferred. Freshers with ITI welding certificate can apply for the trainee role — training given on-site.

Location

Najafgarh, New Delhi. Project sites are spread across Delhi NCR: Delhi, Najafgarh, Dwarka, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.

Pay

Payment is daily wage or monthly salary depending on the role and project. Amount and joining details are discussed directly on phone or WhatsApp.

How to apply

  1. Apply online or on WhatsApp with your name, role and experience.
  2. Phone discussion about the role and your joining date.
  3. Meeting at a project site or our office.
  4. Confirmation and joining, directly with the owner, Subhash Kumar.
