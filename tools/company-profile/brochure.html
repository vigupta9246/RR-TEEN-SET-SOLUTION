Warranty

Standard workmanship warranty on fabrication and installation. Where applicable, the manufacturer warranty on sheeting is passed through to you. Terms are confirmed for each project.

After-sales support

Raise a ticket on the Support page of our website for quality, delay, billing or GST, and warranty issues. Billing queries are usually resolved in 24 to 48 hours and site-visit issues in 3 to 7 days.

Permissions and documents

Most residential and small commercial tin sheds inside your own plot need no separate permission. Large industrial sheds, or rooftop terrace sheds in Delhi, may fall under DDA or MCD norms, and our team guides you on this during planning.

For an industrial shed, keep the plot layout (naksha) and land documents ready. Approvals are discussed at the site visit.

GST and invoicing

A proper GST invoice on every project, useful for B2B and government work. Quoted rates are before GST. GST is 18% for commercial and industrial sheds; some agricultural structures may qualify for a lower rate, confirmed at the site visit.

Payment terms

Typically 50% advance and 50% on completion. Large projects can be milestone-based: booking advance, payment at material delivery and balance on completion.

We accept bank transfer, UPI, NEFT and cheque.
