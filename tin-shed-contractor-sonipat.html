Trusted by manufacturing units along Kundli Industrial Area and the NH-44 belt for tin sheds, warehouses and factory roofing.

Kundli Industrial AreaNH-44 BeltHSIIDC Sonipat

Sonipat sits right on the Delhi border along NH-44, and its Kundli Industrial Area has grown into one of North Delhi NCR's busiest manufacturing clusters — textiles, packaging, auto components and warehousing. R.R Teen Set Solution regularly travels to Sonipat for site visits and has delivered tin shed and steel structure projects for factory owners who wanted a Delhi-based contractor with a proven track record rather than a purely local vendor.

Beyond Kundli, Sonipat district carries a genuinely broad industrial base across its other HSIIDC estates. The Rai Industrial Area in particular has built a strong reputation for household products and steel utensil manufacturing — kitchenware, cutlery, bowls and general steelware — alongside food processing anchored by the large Mega Food Park at Rai. Barhi's industrial estate, developed in phases, adds further general manufacturing capacity along the same NH-44 corridor. And looking ahead, Sonipat's newest growth chapter is unfolding at IMT Kharkhoda, anchored by Maruti Suzuki's large-scale manufacturing complex and a fast-growing base of auto-ancillary suppliers — meaning the district now spans everything from decades-old utensil workshops to some of Haryana's most modern auto-manufacturing infrastructure, often within a short drive of each other.

Why Sonipat Chooses R.R Teen Set Solution

Because Sonipat's Kundli belt has many mid-sized manufacturing units running multiple shifts, our approach focuses on fast turnaround — we prefabricate steel trusses and columns at our workshop and complete on-site erection in days, not weeks. This matters when production can't be paused for long. We also account for Sonipat's proximity to the Yamuna floodplain in some pockets, adjusting foundation and drainage planning where needed.

Sonipat district's administrative history helps explain its current sprawl too — it was carved out as a full-fledged district only in December 1972, taken from Rohtak district, and today comprises three sub-divisions (Ganaur, Sonipat and Gohana) across seven blocks. That relatively young administrative identity, paired with genuinely old industrial estates like Rai dating back decades, means the district has had time to build up a dense and varied manufacturing base without ever consolidating around a single dominant industry — which is precisely why our Sonipat project list stays so mixed, from steel utensil workshops to auto-ancillary units to modern logistics warehousing, often within the same few kilometres.

Common Shed Problems We Solve in Sonipat

Diverse process needs across Rai, Barhi and Kundli: Steel utensil manufacturing, food processing and general auto-ancillary work all have different hygiene, ventilation and load requirements. We assess the specific process at each plot rather than applying one standard spec across the district.

Multi-shift operational wear: Many Sonipat units run continuous shifts, which puts more sustained stress on shed frames and fastening than intermittent-use structures. We size for this level of use from the outset.

Floodplain-adjacent drainage concerns: Similar to neighbouring Kundli and Narela, some Sonipat plots near the Yamuna need extra attention to foundation height and drainage slope to manage monsoon-season risk.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Sonipat

Given the district's process diversity, the type of manufacturing matters significantly — steel utensil and food-processing units generally need cleaner panel detailing than general auto-ancillary or warehousing sheds. Multi-shift operational load is the second major variable, since continuous-use units need more robust framing than standard business-hours operations. For plots near the floodplain, foundation and drainage detailing adds a modest premium but reduces monsoon risk. We confirm exact figures after a site visit.

Given how spread out Sonipat district's industrial base is — from Kundli near the Delhi border to Kharkhoda further west — we plan material delivery routes carefully so a Kundli-side project and a Kharkhoda-side project don't compete for the same delivery window unnecessarily.

Materials We Use for Sonipat Projects

For Rai's steel utensil and food-processing units, we use panel and jointing systems suited to hygiene requirements. For Kundli, Barhi and Kharkhoda-area general manufacturing and auto-ancillary sheds, IS-grade galvanized sheeting on a mild-steel or GI frame sized to the load is standard. Warehouse and logistics structures typically use wider-span PEB framing.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your Sonipat unit's shed frame is sound and simply needs updated roofing, a re-sheet is the more economical option. If you're scaling operations near the growing Kharkhoda auto-ancillary corridor, or the structure shows wear from continuous multi-shift use, a new or reinforced structure is the safer long-term investment. We offer a free assessment either way.
