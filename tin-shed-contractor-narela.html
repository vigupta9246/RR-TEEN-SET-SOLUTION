Serving Narela's DSIDC industrial sectors with factory sheds, warehouse structures and PEB buildings for manufacturing units of every size.

Narela Sectors A–GDSIDC PlotsDelhi-Haryana BorderKundli Corridor

Narela Industrial Area sits right on Delhi's northern edge, close to the Haryana border, and has grown into a major relocation hub for manufacturing units. R.R Teen Set Solution has completed shed and structure work across several of Narela's DSIDC sectors, from single-unit factory sheds to larger warehouse spans for logistics operators using the area's connectivity to the Kundli corridor and NH-44.

Narela's industrial development goes back further than most people realize — DSIIDC was declared a Land Development Agency for Narela as early as 1978, and the complex's first phase covered 612 acres across 1,800 plots, planned with genuine attention to green space, with roughly 50,000 trees planted across the estate and a dedicated central plaza area. That relatively planned, spread-out layout is part of why Narela reads differently from denser industrial pockets closer to central Delhi — wider internal roads, more room around individual plots, and (per DSIIDC's own description) a deliberately environment-friendly design brief from the outset. Narela also shares its origin story with neighbouring Bawana as one of the destinations for Delhi's Supreme Court-mandated industrial relocation scheme, so many businesses here moved from non-conforming residential areas and are still working through structural documentation and infrastructure upgrades as the area matures.

Why Narela Industrial Area Chooses R.R Teen Set Solution

Narela's industrial plots vary widely in size — from compact ancillary units to large logistics and warehousing plots benefiting from NH-44 access. We size our steel structures to match, whether that's a tight-span factory shed or a wide-clear-span warehouse for pallet storage. Every project is IS-grade steel with a GST invoice, and we factor in Narela's proximity to the Yamuna floodplain when planning foundation and drainage requirements.

Narela's ongoing development also includes newer projects like a proposed E-Waste Eco-Park at Holambi Kalan, which points to DSIIDC continuing to expand the industrial complex's scope well beyond its original manufacturing-relocation mandate. For shed and structure work, this evolving mix means we increasingly see enquiries not just from traditional manufacturing units but from newer waste-processing and recycling-adjacent businesses, which often have distinct containment and material-handling requirements we factor into the design.

Common Shed Problems We Solve in Narela

Waterlogging risk on low-lying plots: Narela's proximity to the Yamuna floodplain means some plots are more prone to monsoon waterlogging than others. We plan foundation height and drainage slope specifically to reduce this risk rather than using a standard foundation spec everywhere.

Incomplete documentation on relocated units: Similar to Bawana, several Narela units came through the Supreme Court-mandated relocation scheme and are still finalizing structural paperwork. We provide proper documentation alongside construction work to help close these gaps.

Under-utilized plot layout on older sectors: Some of Narela's earlier-developed plots have shed layouts that don't make efficient use of the generous plot sizing common here. We reassess layout options when clients are planning an expansion or rebuild.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Narela

Given the floodplain proximity, foundation and drainage detailing is a genuine cost factor here that doesn't apply the same way in every NCR location — plots closer to the Yamuna typically need a slightly higher foundation and more robust drainage slope, which adds to base cost but reduces monsoon-season risk considerably. Beyond that, span and load requirement follow standard industrial logic — logistics warehouses need wider clear spans than compact factory units. We confirm final figures after an on-site survey.

We also plan drainage material choices around each plot's specific elevation within Narela rather than a district-wide default, since even plots a few hundred metres apart can face meaningfully different flood risk depending on their position relative to the Yamuna floodplain.

Materials We Use for Narela Projects

For Narela's factory and warehouse sheds, we use IS-grade galvanized or colour-coated steel sheeting on a frame sized to the plot's load requirement, with foundation specifications adjusted for plots closer to the floodplain. Wide-span PEB framing is standard for logistics and warehousing clients needing column-free pallet storage.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your Narela unit's shed frame is sound and drainage has been adequate through past monsoons, a re-sheet is usually the more economical option. If you've experienced waterlogging issues, or the structure came from the original relocation-era construction without proper foundation planning for the site's elevation, a redesigned structure with better drainage is the safer long-term investment.
