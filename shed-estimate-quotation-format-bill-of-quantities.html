Shed estimate and quotation format: what every quote must show

Two quotes for the same shed can differ by lakhs, and the cheaper one is not always the better one. The difference hides in what each quote leaves out. This guide shows the lines a proper shed quotation carries, how an estimate differs from a quotation, and how to check the numbers yourself.

November 20269 min readBy R.R Teen Set Solution, Najafgarh, Delhi
In this article

    When you ask a contractor for a price, you may get a rate, an estimate or a proper quotation, and these are not the same thing. A rate such as "₹190 per sq.ft" tells you almost nothing about what is in it. A good quotation tells you the size, the steel, the sheet, the extras and the tax, so you can compare it with the next one.

    This guide covers the words people use (estimate, quotation, BOQ, invoice), the twelve lines to look for, and two small tools: one to check how much roof sheet a shed needs, and one to check how a quote splits between material and labour. For rates by size, see our cost-by-size guide.

    For a small shed a full BOQ may be one page. For a large shed it may run to several. What matters is that quantity, unit and rate are visible, not that it has a particular layout.

    The twelve lines a shed quotation needs

    Hold any quote against this list. Each line should be there, and each should be specific.

    1. Size and heightLength, width, eave height and ridge height, in feet"About 2,000 sq ft"

    9. Labour and erectionFabrication, erection, crane or lifting equipmentMerged into material

    One missing line is a question, not a verdict. It may simply be forgotten. But if several are missing, you are comparing a full quote with a partial one, and the cheaper total is not a saving.

    Check the roof sheet quantity

    How much sheet does the roof need?

    Enter the shed size and slope. The tool works out the roof surface and the number of sheet pieces, with an allowance for laps and cutting.

    2,016sq.ft of roof surface
    30sheet pieces across the length, per slope

    Roof surface is the plan area stretched by the slope, plus the eave overhang. The cover width is the width one sheet covers after the side lap, so ask your supplier for the real figure. Very long runs may be laid in two pieces with an end lap, which needs more sheet. A check on a quote, not a replacement for the drawing.

    Check how the quote splits

    For a typical tin shed, roughly 65 to 70% of the budget is steel and sheeting material, 18 to 22% is fabrication and erection labour, 5 to 8% is transport, and 5 to 10% is foundation and civil work. Enter two figures from a quote to see where it sits.

    Red flags worth a second look

    1. A price per sq.ft and nothing else. Ask for the lines behind it.
    2. "Gauge" instead of millimetres. Gauge numbers vary by standard. Ask for thickness in mm and the coating in GSM.
    3. No GST line, or "GST extra" with no GSTIN. You need a proper invoice for input credit and for any later claim.
    4. A price far below every other quote. Find out which line has been cut.
    5. A large advance and no stages. Tie payments to work done, such as material delivered, frame erected and handover.
    6. No site visit before the quote. Soil, access and height change the price. See our checklist.
    7. No warranty in writing. A promise that is not written down is hard to claim.

    How to compare two quotes fairly

    Put the two quotes side by side and lay them against the twelve lines. Mark each line as the same, better or missing. Then adjust: if one quote leaves out foundations, add a reasonable figure to it before comparing the totals. Compare per sq.ft only after the specifications match. The sheet guide helps with the line that varies most, the roof sheet itself.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between an estimate and a quotation?

    An estimate is an approximate cost worked out before the details are final, so it can change. A quotation is a firm offer for a defined scope at a stated price, usually valid for a set number of days. Ask for the quotation, with the scope written out, before you pay an advance.

    What should a shed quotation include?

    At least: the size and height, the steel section sizes, the roof sheet thickness in mm and its coating, fasteners and flashing, foundations or base plates, painting, doors and ventilators, labour and transport, a separate GST line, the timeline, the payment stages and the warranty.

    What is a bill of quantities (BOQ)?

    A BOQ is the list of every item in a job with its quantity, unit and rate, such as steel in kg, sheet in sq.m and screws in numbers. It lets you see what you are paying for and compare quotes line by line. Small sheds often get a simpler item list, but the idea is the same.

    How do I check the roof sheet quantity?

    Work out the roof surface from the length, the width and the slope, then add about 10% for laps and cutting. The roof sheet calculator in this article does it for you. If the quote shows a sheet area far below that, ask why.

    What is a fair split between material and labour?

    For a typical tin shed, steel and sheeting material is about 65 to 70% of the budget, fabrication and erection labour about 18 to 22%, transport and handling 5 to 8%, and foundation or civil work 5 to 10%. A quote that is far below the market usually cuts the material share.

    Should GST be included in a shed quotation?

    It should appear as its own line, at 18% for commercial work, with the contractor’s GSTIN. Never accept a quote that merges GST into the rate without saying so, because you cannot then compare it with others.
