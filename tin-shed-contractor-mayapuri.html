Tin shed & steel structures in Mayapuri

Mayapuri is known across Delhi for auto parts, engineering workshops and metal fabrication, along with the heavy trade that grows around them. Phase I and II are busy all day with trucks, forklifts, loading and running machines, and that tough working environment is hard on roofs and structures.

A shed here has to survive more than weather. Dust, vibration, vehicle knocks and constant loading all test the steel, so we pay extra attention to member sizes, bracing, base plates and protective coating. The aim is a building that is still doing its job years after the day it was handed over.

Design thinking

Truss bays built for machines, vehicles and daily wear

A truss shed is a simple, honest structure: a top chord, a bottom chord and diagonals that carry the roof load into the columns. For workshops with moderate spans it gives a strong, economical frame with generous clear height. We detail joints and bracing to resist vibration and choose sections that leave margin for equipment you may add later.

Protection matters as much as strength. Primer and finish coats, galvanised sheets and sealed fasteners slow corrosion in a metal-working area where dust and moisture combine. Base plates are raised on plinths to keep column feet clear of wash water and spilled fluids.

Your quote

What changes your Mayapuri quote

Machines and vehicles decide a lot in Mayapuri. These four items move the rate the most.

Machine and crane loads. Heavy machines or a light crane need stronger columns and footings, which move the rate up.

Vehicle movement. Wide bays and tall openings for trucks need bigger frames and sometimes crash protection at the columns.

Wall cladding. Closing the sides with sheets or panels adds to the quote, but also controls dust and noise.

Working around production. If the shop must keep running, the work is phased bay by bay, which takes longer than a clear plot.

Sample calculation: a 30 × 60 ft industrial workshop shed is 1,800 sq.ft. At ₹200–300 per sq.ft before GST that is roughly ₹3,60,000 to ₹5,40,000. The final figure is confirmed in writing after the survey. Use the shed cost calculator for a quick estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Mayapuri

What does an industrial shed cost in Mayapuri?

Industrial sheds range from about ₹200 to ₹300 per sq.ft before GST, and tin sheds from ₹180 to ₹250. Span, height, sheet type and any wall cladding determine your rate.

Can the shed handle forklifts, trucks and vibration?

Yes, when it is designed for it. We specify heavier sections, bracing and protected base plates in areas where vehicles and machinery operate.

Can you re-sheet my roof without stopping production?

In most cases we can, by working bay by bay and covering machines. We agree the sequence with you before we start.

Do you build wall cladding and shutters too?

Yes. Profile-sheet cladding, rolling shutters and personnel doors are part of our fabrication scope.

Is a truss shed or a PEB better for my unit?

For moderate spans a truss shed is economical. For wide clear floors or heavy cranes a PEB is usually the better choice. We compare both with costs before you decide.

Can you build a shed that carries a small crane?

Yes, if the crane load is given to us in advance. The columns and footings are designed for it from the start, not added afterwards.

How do you protect the steel from corrosion?

We use a primer and finish coat on the frame and galvanised sheets on the roof, and seal fasteners to reduce leaks.

Can you add a mezzanine inside my workshop?

Yes. We plan it around your machines, with the load and head room confirmed before fabrication.
