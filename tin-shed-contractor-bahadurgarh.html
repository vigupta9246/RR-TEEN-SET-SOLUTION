Serving Bahadurgarh's furniture, footwear and manufacturing units just across the Tikri border from Delhi.

Tikri BorderFurniture HubFootwear Industry

Bahadurgarh sits just across the Delhi border via Tikri, and has become a major hub for furniture manufacturing, footwear units and light engineering workshops. Because it's so close to our Najafgarh base, Bahadurgarh is one of the fastest locations for us to reach for a free site visit — often the same day.

Bahadurgarh's industrial identity was shaped by HSIIDC, which developed the town's Sector 16 and 4B industrial estates right on NH-10, the highway linking Delhi with Rohtak and Hissar through Jhajjar district. That location — effectively the first major industrial town you hit leaving Delhi westward — is why several established names, including footwear major Relaxo, have units here alongside hundreds of smaller furniture, laminate and sheet-metal manufacturers. The furniture and laminate cluster in particular has grown dense enough that Bahadurgarh is now a recognized sourcing hub for modular furniture, mezzanine flooring systems and metal door/window fittings sold across NCR. For shed work, this mix means we regularly build for two very different needs on the same street — a furniture unit needing wide-span, dust-controlled floor space, and a footwear or fabrication workshop needing a tougher, load-bearing frame for heavier machinery.

That history matters today because HSIIDC has continued modernizing Bahadurgarh's infrastructure well beyond the original Sector 16/4B footprint — the estate now benefits from Delhi Metro connectivity via the Green Line extension to Bahadurgarh City and Brigadier Hoshiar Singh stations, something few of Delhi's other satellite industrial towns can claim, which has made it noticeably easier for furniture and footwear units here to bring in skilled labour from across NCR. That connectivity, combined with the cluster's scale, is also why we see a steady stream of enquiries from businesses relocating from more congested parts of Delhi specifically to Bahadurgarh, rather than the reverse.

Common Shed Problems We Solve in Bahadurgarh

Dust and material spillage in furniture units: Laminate cutting, sanding and spray-finishing work generates fine dust that settles on inventory if the shed isn't properly sealed and ventilated. We design roofing and wall panel joints to minimize dust ingress while still allowing adequate airflow.

Heavier-than-expected machinery loads: As furniture and footwear units scale up, they often add CNC cutting machines, injection moulding equipment or heavier presses than the original shed was designed for. We assess actual floor and frame load before recommending a re-design rather than assuming the existing structure can simply absorb new equipment.

Access constraints near NH-10 frontage plots: Units directly facing the highway sometimes have narrower internal access than expected once you account for loading bays and vehicle turning radius. We plan shed dimensions around actual usable access, not just plot size on paper.

Metro-corridor construction noise and access changes: With ongoing metro-adjacent development near Bahadurgarh City station, some plots have seen temporary access route changes during construction phases. We coordinate delivery timing around these when relevant to avoid delays.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Bahadurgarh

For furniture and footwear units, the biggest cost driver is floor load rating — a unit running CNC or moulding machinery needs a noticeably stronger frame than one doing manual assembly work, and we size the steel section to the actual equipment rather than a generic default. Dust-control detailing (sealed joints, filtered ventilation) adds modestly to cost but matters for units handling fine laminate dust or leather trimmings. For plots directly on NH-10, access planning for delivery vehicles is the other main variable. We confirm final numbers only after a site visit.

Materials We Use for Bahadurgarh Projects

For furniture and footwear manufacturing sheds, we typically use a mild-steel or GI pipe frame with colour-coated sheeting, choosing joint and panel detailing that limits dust ingress without blocking ventilation. For heavier industrial and warehouse work, we move to IS-grade galvanized sheeting on a stronger frame rated for the specific load the client describes.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your Bahadurgarh unit's existing shed frame is sound but the roofing has degraded from dust and years of use, a re-sheet with better dust-sealing detail is usually the more economical fix. If you're adding heavier machinery than the shed was originally designed for, a new or reinforced structure is the safer route — we offer a free structural assessment before recommending either.
