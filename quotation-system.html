WORK HOURS FOR PRESENT / HALF DAY

A worker's status is decided from the hours between their Time In and Time Out — not a manual choice. Below the Half Day threshold, it's marked Absent (the office can still override any of this manually in the Attendance tab).

ADD SITE / OFFICE LOCATION

When a worker self-marks attendance from their phone, their GPS location is checked against these — within range of any one of them counts as "Verified". Stand at the actual site or office and tap "Use My Current Location" for the easiest, most accurate setup.
