Shed cost by size: what 1,000, 2,000 and 5,000 sq ft really cost

Nobody builds a shed by the square foot in the abstract. You have a plot of 20 by 30, or a unit of 2,000 sq ft, and one question: what will it cost? Here is the size-by-size answer, the sum behind it, and a checker for any quote you are holding.

November 20269 min readBy R.R Teen Set Solution, Najafgarh, Delhi
In this article

    Search for the cost of a shed and you find a rate per square foot. That is a start, but nobody owns a square foot. You own a plot of a certain size, and you want to know what covering it will cost. This guide turns the rates into totals for the sizes people ask about most: 20 by 30, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 5,000 and 10,000 sq.ft.

    All figures below use the same rates as our shed cost calculator and are before 18% GST. They are planning figures for Delhi NCR in 2026. The final price is fixed after a site visit, because soil, height and access change it.

    The sum behind every shed price

    The whole thing is one line: covered area × rate for the shed type + add-ons + GST. Two details trip people up.

    • Covered area is the roof footprint, not the plot. A 30 by 40 ft plot with a 2 ft margin all round gives a shed of 26 by 36 ft, which is 936 sq.ft, not 1,200. If you want the roof to overhang the walls for rain cover, add that to the footprint.
    • The type sets the rate, and the span sets the type. A short span can use light sections and sit in the tin shed range. A wide span needs heavier frames and moves into the industrial or PEB range. Our layout guide shows where that line falls.

    What changes, and what does not, as the shed grows

    People expect the rate per sq.ft to fall sharply with size. It falls a little. Here is why.

    • Steel and sheet scale with area. Twice the floor needs roughly twice the frames, purlins and roof sheet, so most of the bill grows in step with area.
    • Some costs are shared. Getting the crew and tools to site, the drawing, the first crane visit and the site cleanup cost nearly the same for 1,000 sq.ft as for 2,000. Spread over more area, they weigh less per sq.ft.
    • Height and span raise the rate. A shed with a 20 ft eave needs heavier columns than one at 12 ft. A 60 ft span needs deeper rafters than a 30 ft span. These move the rate more than size does.
    • Very small jobs cost more per sq.ft. A 300 sq.ft cover pays the same mobilisation as a larger one, so the rate sits toward the top of the range.

    A useful habit. When comparing quotes, divide each total by the covered area and compare the per sq.ft figures. If one is far below the range for that shed type, ask which line was cut.

    Mistakes people make when sizing a shed

    1. Pricing the plot instead of the roof. Measure the covered footprint, margins removed.
    2. Forgetting height. Tell the contractor the tallest thing that must enter. A taller eave costs more than a longer one.
    3. Building to today’s need. Extending later is cheaper if the first frame is designed to open at one end. Mention it at the quote stage.
    4. Comparing totals, not specifications. Two quotes for 2,000 sq.ft are only comparable if sheet thickness, coating and sections match.
    5. Leaving out foundations and doors. Ask whether civil work and doors are in the price or extra.
    6. Buying on a phone quote alone. A quote given without seeing the site is a guess, so ask for a visit before you pay an advance. See our construction checklist.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a 1000 sq ft shed cost?

    A 1,000 sq ft tin shed costs about ₹1,50,000 to ₹2,20,000 before GST at today’s Delhi NCR rates of ₹150 to ₹220 per sq.ft. An industrial shed of the same size costs about ₹2,00,000 to ₹3,00,000, because it uses heavier sections. Add 18% GST on top.

    How much does a 2000 sq ft shed cost?

    A 2,000 sq ft tin shed comes to about ₹3,00,000 to ₹4,40,000 before GST. If it is a workshop with heavier frames, the industrial shed range of ₹200 to ₹300 per sq.ft gives ₹4,00,000 to ₹6,00,000. A prefab warehouse of that size is ₹4,80,000 to ₹6,80,000.

    What does a 20 by 30 shed cost?

    A 20 by 30 ft shed covers 600 sq ft. At ₹150 to ₹220 per sq.ft a basic tin shed costs about ₹90,000 to ₹1,32,000 before GST. A sliding door or colour-coated sheets add to that, as the add-on prices above show.

    Is a bigger shed cheaper per square foot?

    Within the same shed type, a larger job usually earns a slightly lower rate, because steel and sheets are bought in volume and fixed costs such as transport and mobilisation are shared. But the type matters far more than the size. Going from a tin shed to an industrial frame changes the rate by tens of rupees, while going from 2,000 to 5,000 sq ft changes it by a few.

    Is GST included in these prices?

    No. All prices here are before 18% GST. Ask every contractor to show GST as a separate line, and to give a proper GST invoice.

    Why do two quotes for the same size differ so much?

    Most often the sheet thickness, the coating, the steel section sizes or the height are different. One quote may use 0.40 mm sheet and the other 0.50 mm. Compare the written specification line by line before you compare the totals. Our sheet guide explains what to look for.
