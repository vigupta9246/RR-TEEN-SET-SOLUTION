Common Shed Problems We Solve in Faridabad

Rust from decades of industrial exposure: Faridabad's older manufacturing belt has significant industrial activity, and structures here often show accelerated corrosion at joints and base plates compared to purely residential areas. We assess whether anti-rust treatment can save the existing frame or if replacement is more economical.

Roof leaks in aging Sector 24/25 units: Many sheds in this older industrial belt are 15+ years old with original roofing sheets. Perished washers and worn overlaps are the usual cause — often fixable without a full re-sheet.

Space constraints on older, tighter plots: Unlike newer industrial zones with standardized larger plots, older Faridabad sectors often have narrower, deeper plots. We design sheds and PEB structures that maximize usable covered area within these tighter footprints.

New-build compliance for Palwal Road and Ballabgarh plots: For fresh construction on newer industrial land, we ensure the structure design accounts for standard HSIIDC/municipal norms applicable in the area.

Materials We Use

Given Faridabad's industrial pollution levels and heat, we specify IS-grade galvanized steel for structural frames and either galvanized or colour-coated profile sheets for roofing, chosen based on the specific corrosion exposure at your site. For heavy engineering units around Ballabgarh handling larger equipment loads, we design PEB structures with wider column-free spans to accommodate overhead crane systems and larger machinery footprints. Roofing sheet thickness is also adjusted based on span and wind-load requirements — a wider industrial bay needs a thicker gauge sheet than a small commercial roof to avoid flexing or rattling in strong wind, which is one of the technical specifications we finalize during the site survey rather than quoting blind over the phone.

Repair or New Build? A Quick Way to Decide

For Faridabad's older Sector 24/25 units especially, this is the most common question we get. If less than 25% of your roof area is affected and the structural columns and trusses are still sound (no rust-through, no visible bending), repair or partial re-sheeting is almost always the faster and cheaper option — most of these jobs are completed within a week. If more than half the roof is failing, or the frame itself is compromised, a new structure typically works out more cost-effective over a 5-10 year horizon than repeated patch repairs. We give you an honest assessment during the free site visit rather than defaulting to a new-build recommendation.
