Tin shed & steel structures in Jhilmil

Jhilmil Industrial Area in Shahdara is a dense, hardworking estate of small manufacturers, with packaging, plastics, garment and assembly units close to GT Road and Dilshad Garden. Plots are small, buildings stand shoulder to shoulder, and every square foot is expected to earn its keep.

We build for that reality: compact factory sheds that use the plot fully, mezzanines that double the floor, and front openings sized for the vehicles that actually visit. When land is tight the smartest investment is usually height, and a steel frame is the fastest way to add it.

Design thinking

Making a small Jhilmil plot work harder with height

On a small plot the floor is the scarce resource, so we plan upward. A single high-bay shed with a mezzanine along one side lets production run below while storage sits above, joined by a steel staircase placed where it wastes the least space.

Neighbouring buildings are close, so we plan the work to be tidy: pre-cut members from our workshop, quick erection and careful debris handling. Roof slopes and gutters send rainwater the right way so it does not become your neighbour's problem. Our tin shed types guide helps you pick the right sheet and frame.

Your quote

What changes your Jhilmil quote

Small plots are priced differently from big ones. These four items matter most in Jhilmil.

Plot size and height. Small plots are mostly priced by height and number of openings, not just area.

Mezzanine and shutters. These are extras to the shed rate and are quoted separately so you can add them when you need.

Narrow lane access. Limited access means smaller fabricated pieces and more joints on site.

Wall closing. Cladding all sides adds cost, but gives a secure, weather-proof unit.

Quick example: a 20 × 40 ft small factory shed is 800 sq.ft. At ₹185–280 per sq.ft before GST that is roughly ₹1,48,000 to ₹2,24,000. The final figure is confirmed in writing after the survey. Use the shed cost calculator for a quick estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Jhilmil

What does a small factory shed cost in Jhilmil?

Tin sheds start from about ₹180 per sq.ft and go up to ₹250 before GST, and prefab warehouses range from ₹185 to ₹280. A quick estimate is available on our shed cost calculator.

Can you add a mezzanine inside my existing shed?

Often yes, after checking the columns and floor. A steel mezzanine is designed for your storage load, with decking and a staircase.

Can you fit roller shutters?

Yes. We supply and fit roller shutters and profile-sheet fronts as part of the shed.

How long does a small shed take?

Small sheds up to 1,000 sq.ft usually take 7–12 days from approval.

Do you serve Ghaziabad too?

Yes. Jhilmil borders Ghaziabad, and our Ghaziabad contractor page has details for projects across the border.

Can a small plot get both a mezzanine and a shutter?

Yes. We plan the height so a mezzanine, shutter and headroom all fit, and we show you the layout before fabrication.

Do you handle roof repair for older Jhilmil sheds?

Yes. We can re-sheet, replace damaged purlins and seal leaks, instead of building a new shed.

Can you build in stages as my business grows?

Yes. We can design the frame so another bay can be added later without disturbing the existing one.
