Steel structures for compact Mangolpuri plots

Many Mangolpuri units are small plots with tall roofs and a floor that is already full. Instead of buying more land, the practical way to grow is up. A steel mezzanine turns the empty height under the roof into a second working floor for packing, stock, finishing or a small office.

We design the mezzanine around the building you already have. Columns are placed so machines and doorways stay clear, beams are sized for the load you plan to keep on the deck, and most steel is fabricated in our workshop so the work inside your shed stays short and tidy.

How the structure works

How a steel mezzanine carries its load

A mezzanine is a small steel building inside a larger one. Columns stand on base plates, main beams span between them, closely spaced joists sit across the beams, and a deck plate forms the floor. Load travels from the deck, to the joists, to the beams, to the columns and finally to the ground.

The two numbers we ask for first are the weight you will store per square metre and the head room you need below. From these we choose the beam depth and the joist spacing. A railing, a staircase and a safe edge are part of the design, not an afterthought, because people will work at height.

Materials

Decking and steel choices for Mangolpuri mezzanines

Chequered plate or sheet deck

Chequered plate is tough and slip-resistant for heavy foot traffic. A lighter profile deck with a cover layer suits lighter loads.

Beam and joist sizing

Heavier stock needs deeper beams and tighter joists. We match them to the weight you give us, with margin.

Primer and finish coat

The steel is primed and finished in our workshop, so the work on your floor is mostly bolting.

Your quote

What changes your Mangolpuri quote

Load per square metre. A deck for light cartons costs less than one for rolls or machines. The load decides the steel.

Area of the deck. A bigger deck needs more beams, joists and plates, and that raises the total.

Height and stairs. A taller mezzanine needs longer columns and a longer staircase.

Existing floor condition. If the slab needs strengthening at base-plate locations, we include it in the plan.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Mangolpuri

How much load can a mezzanine carry?

That depends on the beam and joist design. We ask for the weight per square metre you will store and design the deck for it with margin. We do not guess the load.

Can a mezzanine be built inside a working shed?

Yes. Most steel is fabricated in our workshop, so installation inside the shed is mainly lifting, bolting and decking. We schedule around your work hours.

Do I need a new floor for a mezzanine?

Not always. We check the slab at the base-plate positions and tell you if it needs strengthening.

What is the head room below a mezzanine?

We decide it with you. For forklifts and trolleys we usually plan generous clear height, and we confirm it in the drawing.

Can you add a staircase and railing?

Yes. A staircase, handrails and an edge guard are part of every mezzanine we plan.

What does a shed cost in Mangolpuri?

A tin shed costs from about ₹180 to ₹250 per sq.ft before GST and an industrial shed ₹200–300. A mezzanine is quoted from its own drawing.

Can you also build a new factory shed here?

Yes. We build tin sheds, truss sheds and warehouses on plots across North-West Delhi.

Will the mezzanine need approval?

Rules differ by building and use. We tell you what to check early so you can talk to your own architect or the local authority before work begins.
