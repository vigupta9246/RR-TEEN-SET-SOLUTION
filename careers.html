FAQ

Questions From Candidates

Is training provided for freshers?

Yes. Helper roles need no prior experience — full on-the-job training is given. Freshers with an ITI welding certificate can also apply for the trainee welder position and are trained on-site by our experienced welders.

Is payment daily wage or monthly salary?

It depends on the role and project — we discuss daily wage or monthly salary directly on the call or at the first meeting, so there's no confusion before you join.

Do I need to bring my own tools?

Basic hand tools are useful if you already have them, but all major welding and fabrication equipment is provided by the company.

Which areas will I be working in?

Project sites are spread across Delhi, Najafgarh, Dwarka, Noida, Gurugram, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and surrounding Delhi NCR — you'll usually know your site location a few days in advance.

How does the hiring process work?

It's a short, direct process: send your details via the form or WhatsApp, a quick phone discussion about the role and your experience, then a meeting at an ongoing site or our office before confirming joining — no lengthy interview rounds.
