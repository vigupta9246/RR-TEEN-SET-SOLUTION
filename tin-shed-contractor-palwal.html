Palwal sits on the Delhi-Mathura Road (NH-19) and is home to IMT Palwal, an industrial model township that's grown steadily — a good share of our project work here is factory and warehouse sheds for units there. Palwal's surrounding areas are also heavily agricultural, so we regularly build godown and storage sheds for local farmers and traders, alongside residential rooftop work.

Palwal's industrial profile is on the verge of a major shift. Beyond the existing IMT Palwal township, the Haryana government has begun acquiring roughly 9,000 acres across nine villages spanning Faridabad and Palwal districts — including Mohiyapur, Baghpur Kalan, Baghpur Khurd, Baharaula, Hansapur, Solda and Thanthri on the Palwal side — to build a new HSIIDC-led industrial city along the Greenfield Expressway connecting the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway to Noida International Airport. The area also sits at a genuinely strategic transport junction: the KMP/KGP Expressway already links Palwal to Kundli, Manesar and Ghaziabad, and the upcoming Haryana Orbital Rail Corridor will run between Palwal and Sonipat via Manesar. For businesses and landowners here, this means Palwal is shifting from a secondary NCR industrial pocket to what's being positioned as one of the largest integrated industrial hubs in the region — which is already translating into more enquiries for both new-build factory sheds and expansion of existing units anticipating this growth.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Palwal

For IMT Palwal industrial units, load requirement and clear span drive most of the cost, following the same logic as other industrial belts. For agricultural godowns, moisture-control detailing and storage capacity matter more than raw structural strength. Given Palwal's upcoming industrial city development, businesses planning near-term expansion sometimes opt for slightly stronger foundations upfront to support future extension — this is optional but worth discussing during the site visit if growth is on your roadmap.

For clients planning around Palwal's upcoming industrial city development, we're also happy to discuss slightly over-specced foundation and frame choices upfront, which can save on retrofit costs later if expansion plans firm up sooner than expected.

Materials We Use for Palwal Projects

For IMT Palwal factory and warehouse sheds, we use IS-2062 grade structural steel with anti-corrosion treatment, sized to the client's load and process. For agricultural godowns, galvanized sheeting with proper roof venting is standard, with insulated panel options available for produce needing tighter temperature control.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your Palwal shed's frame is structurally sound and simply needs updated roofing, a re-sheet is the more economical option. If you're planning to expand ahead of Palwal's industrial growth, or the structure can't support your current storage or production needs, a new or reinforced structure is the better investment. We offer a free assessment either way.
