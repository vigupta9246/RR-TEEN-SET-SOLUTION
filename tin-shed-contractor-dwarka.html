Common Shed Problems We Solve in Dwarka

Terrace water seepage: This is the single most common call we get from Dwarka homeowners. Delhi's monsoon combined with flat RCC terraces often leads to seepage into the top floor. A properly sloped tin or profile-sheet rooftop shed diverts water away entirely, which is usually a more permanent and cheaper fix than repeated waterproofing coats.

Housing society car parking shortage: Many RWAs and cooperative societies in Dwarka's older sectors were built before car ownership rose to today's levels. We install multi-bay car parking sheds that add covered parking without major civil work, since the structure is bolted rather than foundation-heavy in most cases.

Shop and commercial shutters needing weather protection: Shopkeepers in sector markets often need a simple extended roofing structure over their shopfront — we handle these as quick, low-disruption jobs since most sector markets have limited working hours for construction noise.

DDA compliance concerns: A common worry we hear is whether a shed will violate society or DDA norms. We design within standard permissible height and setback limits used across Dwarka's residential sectors, so structures don't create compliance issues later.

What Affects Your Final Quote in Dwarka

Terrace access is a bigger factor here than in open industrial plots — many Dwarka buildings only have a single narrow staircase to the terrace, which affects how material is carried up and can add slightly to labour time. Roof span and whether you want insulation (to reduce summer heat on the top floor) are the other two main cost drivers. For car parking sheds, the number of bays and whether the structure needs to attach to an existing boundary wall or stand independently both affect the final number. We only confirm exact pricing after seeing the site in person.

Materials We Use for Dwarka Projects

For residential rooftop and car parking work, we typically use galvanized or colour-coated profile sheets over a mild-steel or GI pipe frame, chosen for a balance of durability and appearance since these structures are visible from inside the home or society premises. For insulated rooftop sheds — increasingly popular given Delhi's summer heat — we use PUF (polyurethane foam) sandwich panels that noticeably reduce the top floor's indoor temperature compared to a bare RCC terrace or basic tin sheet.

Repair or New Shed? A Quick Way to Decide

If your existing rooftop shed is more than 8-10 years old and showing rust at the joints or sheet edges, it's worth getting it inspected before assuming a full replacement is needed — many older Dwarka rooftop sheds just need a re-sheet or sealant work at the overlaps. If the frame itself has bent or rusted through, or if you're expanding coverage (adding a car parking bay, for instance), a new structure is usually the better long-term investment. We offer a free inspection to give you an honest recommendation either way, not just a sales pitch for a new build.
