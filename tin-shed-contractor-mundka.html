Tin shed & steel structures in Mundka

Mundka Industrial Area sits on the Delhi–Haryana corridor along Rohtak Road, close to Nangloi, Tikri Kalan and Bahadurgarh. Plastics, packaging, small manufacturing and a large number of godowns and logistics units are spread across its plots, and trucks are part of daily life here.

That is why we start every Mundka project with the vehicle and the stock, not the roof. How a truck enters and turns, how high the racking goes and where an emergency exit must stay clear decide the frame. A good shed here is one your team can load, unload and leave quickly without friction.

Design thinking

Planning a Mundka warehouse around trucks, racking and exits

A pallet racking system fixes the clear height, bay width and floor load of a warehouse. We work backwards from your racks and forklift type, then choose eave height, column grid and frame so the building suits the way you store, not the other way round. A prefab or PEB frame gives wide column-free spans at a quicker pace than conventional construction.

Fire safety needs early attention in storage buildings. Exit paths, door positions, clear aisles and sheet choice affect how safely a building performs, and requirements depend on size, height and use. We build to your approved plan and recommend confirming fire requirements with your architect and the authority before the layout is finalised.

Roofing and cladding for a Mundka warehouse

A warehouse roof works hard in summer, so the skin of the building matters as much as the frame.

Profile sheets with insulation

Warehouses hold heat in summer. Sheets with a PUF or bubble-foil layer keep stored goods cooler and lower the pressure on cooling.

PEB frames for long spans

Pre-engineered frames give a wide, column-free floor, which suits forklifts and racking. Check our PEB page to see when they beat a conventional truss.

Wall cladding and shutters

Single-skin or sandwich wall panels, with roller shutters sized for your trucks, close the building neatly and keep dust out.

Your quote

What changes your Mundka quote

A Mundka warehouse quote changes mostly with how the building will be used. These four items matter most.

Clear span and eave height. Taller eaves for racking and wider spans need heavier frames, which is the biggest change in rate.

Docks and openings. Every loading dock, shutter and personnel door adds framing and finishing work.

Floor and foundations. Heavy racking and truck movement need a floor and footings designed for the load, which sit outside the shed rate.

Insulation and fire planning. Insulated roofs, smoke vents and extra exits add to the quote, and they are best decided at the start.

Worked example: a 50 × 100 ft warehouse bay is 5,000 sq.ft. At ₹185–280 per sq.ft before GST that is roughly ₹9,25,000 to ₹14,00,000. The final figure is confirmed in writing after the survey. Use the shed cost calculator for a quick estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Mundka

What does a warehouse shed cost in Mundka?

Prefab warehouse sheds start from about ₹185 per sq.ft and go up to ₹280 per sq.ft before GST, while industrial sheds range from ₹200 to ₹300. Span, height, insulation and shutters change where your quote falls.

How big a clear span can you build for a warehouse?

PEB structures can span up to 90 m column-free. Most Mundka warehouses need much less, and we choose the span from your rack layout and budget.

Can you extend an existing shed while we keep working?

In most cases, yes. We survey the old structure, plan the connection and phase the work so operations continue in the unaffected part of the building.

How long does a prefab warehouse take?

Small and medium PEB projects typically take 25–55 days depending on span and size, while a tin shed up to 1,000 sq.ft is usually ready in 7–12 days.

Do you help with fire-safety planning?

We build to your approved drawing and can suggest layouts with clear exits and aisles, but formal fire requirements depend on size, height and use, so please confirm them with your architect and the authority.

Can you build a warehouse with a loading dock in Mundka?

Yes. We plan the dock height, apron and shutter openings together with the frame so trucks can reverse in and unload without clashing with columns.

Is a PEB or a conventional truss better for a Mundka warehouse?

For long clear spans and quick erection a PEB is often better. For smaller bays a conventional truss can be cheaper. We compare both after the survey.

Can the warehouse have an office or mezzanine inside?

Yes. A steel mezzanine can be designed for an office or extra storage, with the loads agreed upfront.
