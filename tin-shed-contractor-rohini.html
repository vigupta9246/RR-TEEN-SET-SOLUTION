Terrace and rooftop sheds in Rohini

Rohini is mostly planned sectors of multi-storey homes and shops. Roofs are open terraces, plots are tight, and parking is always short. A well-built steel shed turns an unused terrace into a covered room for storage, a utility space, a small workshop or a place to dry clothes through the monsoon.

Because the building is already there, the shed has to be light and carefully fixed. We use slim steel columns, a single-slope roof for fast water run-off and good fixing to the structure. The aim is a tidy terrace room that looks neat from the street and does not overload the building.

How the structure works

Building a light shed on top of an existing house

A terrace shed starts with its footing on the roof slab. Steel base plates are fixed at the column lines so the load spreads over the slab and, where possible, over the existing beams and columns below. The columns carry rafters, the rafters carry purlins and the purlins carry the sheets.

The roof is a single slope, which makes the frame simple and drains water to one side. We keep the steel light and the slope matched to your parapet and water outlet. If your building is old, we say so early and recommend a structural check, because the shed is only as safe as what holds it up.

Your quote

What changes your Rohini quote

Area and height. A larger or taller shed needs more steel and sheets.

Sheet type. Colour-coated and insulated sheets cost more than plain GI.

Side cladding. Closing the sides adds cost but gives a usable room.

Access to the roof. A difficult lift or staircase may add time and labour.

Worked example: an 18 × 25 ft terrace shed is 450 sq.ft. At ₹180–250 per sq.ft before GST that is roughly ₹81,000 to ₹1,12,500. The final figure is confirmed in writing after the survey. Try the shed cost calculator for a quick estimate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Rohini

How much does a terrace shed cost in Rohini?

A rooftop or terrace tin shed is usually from ₹180 to ₹250 per sq.ft before GST. Height, sheet type and side cladding set your figure, and we confirm it in writing after the survey.

Will the terrace shed overload my building?

We keep the frame light and fix it where the structure can carry it. If your building is old, we recommend a structural check before work.

Can the shed be fully closed?

Yes. We can clad the sides and add a door, so the shed becomes a usable room.

How do you avoid leakage?

The roof slopes to an outlet, the sheet laps are sealed and fasteners use proper washers. See our monsoon waterproofing guide.

How long does a terrace shed take?

Most terrace sheds take about one to two weeks after drawings are approved.

Do you build parking canopies too?

Yes. We build single and double-car canopies shaped to your gate and frontage.

Can you keep the terrace cooler?

Light-coloured or insulated sheets and a small air gap can help. We suggest options for your use.

Do I need society or authority permission?

Rules vary by building and society. We tell you what to check early so you can get any permission you need before work.
