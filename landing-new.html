Step 2 of 6

Columns

Steel columns are cut and welded to the drawing, bolted to base plates and checked for plumb. Each one carries roof load down, and wind uplift up, to its footing.

Ask your contractorWhat section and thickness are the columns, and how big is the base plate?
Parts of a shed frame
Step 3 of 6

Rafters

Rafters run from column to ridge. The slope is set so rain leaves fast. Most sheet roofs use a slope of 1 in 10 or steeper.

Ask your contractorWhat roof slope are you proposing, and which rafter section?
Step 4 of 6

Purlins and bracing

Purlins carry the sheets, and their spacing decides how thick the sheet must be. The red diagonals are bracing. They keep the frame square when a storm hits.

Ask your contractorWhat is the purlin spacing, and where is the bracing?

Sheeting

Sheets are screwed to the purlins with sealed laps. Thickness, coating and paint decide how long the roof lasts, so all three belong in writing.

What a good shed quote says

Two quotes for the same size can hide very different sheds. Check that these seven lines are written down before you compare prices.

  • Size and height. Length, width and eave height in feet.
  • Sheet thickness in mm. And whether it is steel only (BMT) or coated total (TCT).
  • Coating and paint. Coating class such as Z120, Z150 or AZ150, and the paint system.
  • Purlin spacing and roof slope. These tell you how stiff the roof will be.
  • Footing size and depth. Based on a visit to your site, not a guess.
  • GST shown separately. 18% on its own line, so quotes compare fairly.
  • Warranty terms. Complaints attended within 2 working days of your call or WhatsApp.
