PEB and steel structures in Patparganj

East Delhi plots near Patparganj are often tight, with neighbours on both sides and a narrow frontage. That makes build time and site mess matter as much as cost. A pre-engineered building suits this well, because the main frames are cut, welded and coated in a workshop and arrive ready to bolt together.

On site, the work is mostly lifting and bolting. That means fewer weeks of noise, cleaner plots and fewer surprises. We design the frames for the span and loads you give us, and the building is delivered with the anchor bolts and fixings it needs.

How the frame works

Why PEB frames are quick to put up

A PEB main frame is a portal: two tapered columns and two tapered rafters that meet at a knee and at the ridge. The members are deeper where the bending is higher and slimmer where it is lower, which saves steel. Secondary members such as purlins and girts connect the frames and carry the sheets.

Because the parts are produced to measure, site work is mostly erection. Anchor bolts are set in the foundation, the frames are lifted one by one, and rod bracing keeps the structure square. Roof and wall sheets are then fixed to the purlins and girts. A ridge ventilator helps hot air escape.

Your quote

What changes your Patparganj quote

Span and eave height. Larger spans and taller eaves need heavier frames.

Crane or hoist load. Any crane load needs stronger columns and brackets and adds to the cost.

Sheeting and insulation. Insulated roofs and walls cost more than single-skin.

Doors and openings. More and larger doors add framing and trim.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Patparganj

What is the PEB rate in Patparganj?

PEB structures usually fall between ₹220 and ₹380 per sq.ft before GST. Span, height, loads and sheeting decide your figure, and we confirm it in writing after the survey.

Is PEB faster than conventional steel?

Usually yes, because the members are made in a workshop and bolted up on site. We give a build time in the quote.

Can a PEB be extended later?

Yes. Plan an end bay at the start, and we add more frames without removing the existing ones.

Can PEB handle a crane?

Yes, if the crane load and runway details are given at design time. We size the columns and brackets for it.

Do you provide drawings before fabrication?

Yes. You get a plan and elevation drawing so you can approve the span, height and openings.

How do you keep the hall cool in summer?

Insulated sheets and a ridge ventilator both help. We suggest the mix based on how you will use the building.

Can you work on a plot with a narrow frontage?

Yes. We plan crane and truck positions in advance and deliver parts in manageable sizes.

Do you repair or re-sheet old PEB roofs?

Yes. We re-sheet, seal and re-fix roofs. See our roofing contractor page.
