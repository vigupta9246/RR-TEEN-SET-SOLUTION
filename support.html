How Our Support System Works

Whether you're in the middle of a project or it's been completed for a while, this page is the fastest way to reach us for anything that needs tracking — a quality concern, a delay, a billing question, or a warranty claim. Every ticket you raise here goes directly into our system with a unique Ticket ID, so nothing gets lost in a WhatsApp thread or a missed call, and you can check progress yourself anytime instead of waiting on a callback.

Raising a New Ticket

  1. Fill in your name, phone number and a short description of the issue on the "Raise New Ticket" tab above.
  2. Pick the category that best matches your issue — Quality Issue, Delay, Billing, General Query, or Other.
  3. If your issue relates to a specific project, add the quotation or invoice ID so our team can pull up the right file instantly.
  4. Submit, and you'll immediately get a Ticket ID — save this, since it's what you'll use to track status later.

Tracking an Existing Ticket

Switch to the "Track My Ticket" tab and enter your Ticket ID along with the same phone number you used when raising it — this combination verifies it's really you before showing any details. Once verified, you'll see the current status (Open, In Progress, Resolved or Closed) and the full conversation thread with our support team, and you can add more information or reply directly from there if we need anything further from you.

What You Can Raise a Ticket For

  • Quality Issue: Concerns about fabrication finish, sheeting alignment, welding, or anything about the physical structure that doesn't look or feel right.
  • Delay: If a scheduled site visit, installation, or material delivery is running behind what was communicated.
  • Billing / GST Invoice: Questions about your quote, final invoice, GST details, or payment records.
  • Warranty Claim: Rust, leakage, or structural issues on a completed project that falls within the workmanship warranty period.
  • General Query: Anything else — from asking about maintenance to planning a future expansion of an existing structure.

Our Response & Resolution Process

Once a ticket is submitted, our team reviews it and updates the status as work progresses. Billing and general queries are usually resolved over a message exchange within 24-48 hours. Quality or warranty issues that require us to physically inspect the structure are scheduled as a free site visit, typically within 3-7 days depending on the nature of the problem and your location within Delhi NCR. You'll see every update reflected on this page in real time, so there's no need to keep calling for a status check.

Warranty & After-Sales Support

R.R Teen Set Solution provides a standard workmanship warranty on fabrication and installation across all our projects — tin sheds, industrial sheds, prefab warehouses, and PEB structures alike. Where the sheeting or steel material carries a manufacturer warranty, we pass that through to you as part of your project documentation. If you notice an issue you believe falls under warranty — rust at joints, sheet displacement, or a structural concern — raising a ticket here with your invoice ID is the quickest way to get it assessed. For general upkeep in between, our tin shed maintenance guide covers what to check seasonally, especially before Delhi's monsoon.

Frequently Asked Questions — Support

How do I raise a support ticket with R.R Teen Set Solution?

Use the "Raise New Ticket" tab on this page, fill in your name, phone number, category and issue description, and submit. You'll receive a unique Ticket ID to track progress.
