FAQ

Warehouse Questions

Prefab warehouse banwane mein kitna kharcha aata hai Delhi mein?

Delhi NCR mein prefab warehouse ka rate ₹185 se ₹280/sq.ft hota hai. 5000 sq.ft ka medium warehouse approx ₹9–12 lakh mein tayaar ho jaata hai. Cold storage structure thoda mahanga hota hai — ₹12–16 lakh for 5000 sq.ft. Contact karein free quote ke liye.

Kya warehouse mein forklift chal sakti hai?

Haan! Hum forklift-compatible warehouses banate hain — RCC flooring recommendation ke saath aur adequate clear height (7–12m) ke saath. Loading bays aur dock levelers bhi add kar sakte hain.

Delivery kitne din mein hogi?

Small warehouse (up to 3000 sq.ft) 15–20 din mein, medium (3000–10,000 sq.ft) 20–35 din mein, aur large warehouse 35–55 din mein tayaar ho jaata hai. Written timeline commitment dete hain.

Warehouse construction company Delhi NCR mein kaise select karein?

GST-registered company choose karein jo written material specification aur timeline commitment de. Loading bay, flooring aur ventilation requirement clearly discuss karein site visit ke waqt — isse baad mein confusion nahi hota.

Warehouse mein loading bay aur dock leveler add ho sakta hai?

Haan, design stage mein hi loading bay, dock leveler aur wide entry gate plan kiya ja sakta hai. Ye truck/container access ke liye zaroori hote hain, isliye pehle hi requirement bata dena best rehta hai.

Prefab warehouse aur PEB structure mein kya fark hai?

Prefab warehouse ek specific use-case hai (storage/logistics ke liye), jabki PEB ek broader construction method hai jo factory, warehouse aur showroom — sabke liye use hota hai. Dono mein steel framing technology same hoti hai. Dekhein hamara PEB structure page detailed comparison ke liye.
