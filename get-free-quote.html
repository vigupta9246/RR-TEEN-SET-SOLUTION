FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer a free site visit?

Yes! We offer a free site visit anywhere in Delhi NCR, including Delhi, Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad and Ghaziabad. No charge, no obligation. Call us to fix a date.

How soon will I get the quote?

You will receive a detailed quotation on WhatsApp within 24 hours of the site visit. Same-day quotes are possible for urgent projects.

What is the minimum project size?

We accept projects from 100 sq.ft to 1,00,000+ sq.ft, from small residential sheds to large industrial complexes.

What are the payment terms?

Typically 50% advance and 50% on completion. Large projects can use milestone-based payments. We accept bank transfer, UPI, NEFT and cheque.

Will I get a GST invoice?

Yes, absolutely. GST No: 07DJCPK0635M1Z5. We issue a proper GST invoice on every project, suitable for B2B and government tenders.
