Delhi mein tin shed banane ka kharcha kitna hai?

Delhi NCR mein 2026 mein tin shed ka kharcha approximately ₹150–220 per square feet hota hai. 1000 sqft ke liye ₹1.5L–₹2.2L, 2000 sqft ke liye ₹3L–₹4.4L, 5000 sqft ke liye ₹7.5L–₹11L lagta hai. Free quote ke liye call karein: +91 85272 58462.

Industrial shed cost per sqft Delhi NCR 2026 mein kya hai?

Delhi NCR mein industrial shed ka cost ₹200–300 per square feet hota hai. 5000 sqft ke liye ₹10L–₹15L ka budget rakhein. Isme heavy steel frame, colour-coated sheets aur standard fittings included hain. GST @18% alag se lagegi.

PEB structure aur tin shed mein kya difference hai?

Tin shed ₹150–220/sqft — chhote areas ke liye best, jaldi ban jaati hai. PEB ₹280–380/sqft — large spans (50ft+) ke liye better, zyada durable aur long-term mein economical. 30,000 sqft+ projects ke liye PEB recommend hota hai.

Shed banane mein kitna time lagta hai?

Tin shed 7–15 din, industrial shed 15–30 din, PEB structure 3–6 hafte. R.R Teen Set Solution ne 500+ projects on-time complete kiye hain — time pe delivery guarantee ke saath.
