Okhla Industrial Estate

Tin shed & steel structures in Okhla

Okhla Industrial Estate is one of Delhi's older planned estates, with garment, printing, packaging and light engineering units packed into compact plots across three phases. Most plots here are already built up, so the question is rarely where to put a brand-new shed and almost always how to add usable space to what you already have.

That is the problem we solve in Okhla. We add rooftop sheds, terrace extensions, mezzanine floors and re-sheeted roofs to existing buildings, working carefully around the structure that is already there. For a small unit, a covered terrace or an extra storage floor can be worth more than a bigger plot ever could.

Area snapshot

Three ways Okhla units add space

Rooftop & terrace sheds

Light steel trusses and profile sheets turn an open terrace into covered storage, a packing area or a utility floor, without the weight and time of a new concrete slab.

Mezzanine floors

A steel mezzanine adds usable area inside a tall shed. It is designed for your actual load, whether that is cartons, fabric rolls or light machinery.

Re-sheeting & repair

Ageing roofs on older Okhla buildings leak at laps and fasteners. We strip, re-sheet and re-seal, adding insulation or ventilators where heat is the problem.

Design thinking

Adding space to a built-up Okhla plot without overloading it

Before any steel goes on an existing building, the slab, beams and columns must be able to carry it. We look at how the building was made, mark where new columns can land and spread the load so the new shed sits on the strongest parts of the structure. If a point is doubtful we say so and suggest your structural engineer verify it.

Lightweight steel is the reason rooftop and mezzanine work is practical. A truss roof with profile sheets weighs a fraction of a concrete slab, goes up in days rather than weeks and can be extended or dismantled later. For heat-sensitive work we recommend insulated sheets or PUF panels and ridge ventilation; our insulation guide explains the options.

Materials

Light, safe materials for Okhla rooftops

The right material depends on the use, the budget and how the building looks from the road. Here is how we usually think about it for Okhla.

Light steel trusses

On an older building every kilo counts. Light trusses and slim purlins keep the added load low while still giving a strong roof.

Insulated roof sheets

A roof exposed to direct sun heats the floor below. Insulated sheets or ventilators make the top floor easier to work in.

Mezzanine decking

Steel beams with plate or sheet decking give a floor for cartons or light machinery, sized to the load you plan to keep on it.

Your quote

What changes your Okhla quote

The rate per sq.ft is a range, because no two plots are the same. These four things move it most.

Building condition. An old structure may need a check before loading, which can change how the new frame is supported.

Access and lifting. Tight lanes and no crane access mean parts are lifted by hand or in smaller pieces, which adds time.

Mezzanine load and height. The weight you will store and the head room underneath decide the beam size and the final rate.

Roof repair scope. Re-sheeting only is cheaper than a new frame. We tell you which one your roof actually needs.

Illustration only: a 20 × 30 ft terrace shed is 600 sq.ft. At ₹180–250 per sq.ft before GST that is roughly ₹1,08,000 to ₹1,50,000. Your actual figure depends on the factors above and is confirmed in writing after the site survey. Use the shed cost calculator for a quick estimate.

Before the survey

What to keep ready for your Okhla site visit

You do not need drawings to start. A few details help us give a sharper written quote for your rooftop shed or mezzanine.

Plot size and photos. Approximate length and width, plus a few photos or a WhatsApp location pin.

Use of the building. What will be stored, parked or made inside, and any machines, racks or vehicles.

Height and openings. The clear height you need and the number and size of doors or shutters.

Timeline and access. When you want it ready, and how a truck or crane can reach the plot.

Not sure about any of these? Leave it to us. We measure and confirm everything at the free survey.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions — Okhla

Can you build a rooftop shed on an old Okhla building?

Often yes, after a structural check. We look at the slab and columns, plan where loads can land and suggest your structural engineer confirm it. Lightweight steel trusses keep the added weight low.

How much does a mezzanine floor cost in Okhla?

Cost depends on the floor load, span, decking and access, so it is quoted after a site visit. For reference, our PEB structures run ₹220–380 per sq.ft and tin sheds ₹180–250 per sq.ft, before GST.

Will rooftop work disturb production on the floors below?

We sequence the work to keep the floors below running, and plan welding and lifting to limit noise, sparks and debris.

Can an old leaking roof be repaired instead of replaced?

Sometimes. If the frame is sound we re-sheet, replace fasteners and seal the laps. If the frame is badly corroded we recommend partial or full replacement. See our roof leakage repair guide.

How long does a terrace shed take?

A terrace shed of up to 1,000 sq.ft generally takes 7–12 days, depending on access and design.

Do you check the old building before adding a rooftop shed?

We look at the columns, slab and how the load will travel before we design the frame. If the building needs a structural engineer's opinion we say so before work begins.

Can a mezzanine be built in a short working day window?

Often yes. Most steel parts are cut and welded in our workshop, so the work on site is mainly lifting, bolting and decking.

Can you add insulation to an existing hot roof?

Yes. We can re-sheet with insulated panels or add insulation under existing sheets, depending on the roof condition.
