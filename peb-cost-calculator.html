    What moves a PEB estimate up or down

    Two buildings with the same floor area can differ a lot in price. These four choices matter most, and you control all of them in the calculator.

    Clear span

    Wider column-free space needs deeper steel frames. Beyond about 30 m, each extra 10 m of span adds roughly 8 to 12 percent.

    Eave height

    Standard eave height is 6 to 8 m. Going above 10 m means taller columns and more wall sheeting, so the rate climbs.

    Bay spacing

    About 6 m between frames is the most economical. Wider bays need heavier purlins and push cost up.

    Cladding and crane

    PUF sandwich panels cost several times more than plain GI, and a crane needs a stronger frame. Both change the rate noticeably.

    PEB cost questions, answered

    What is the PEB cost per sq ft in Delhi NCR in 2026?

    PEB structure cost typically runs from about Rs 250 to Rs 600 per sq ft for the steel frame with roof and wall sheeting. Small buildings up to 5,000 sq ft are around Rs 250 to 320, medium ones of 5,000 to 20,000 sq ft around Rs 280 to 380, large ones above 20,000 sq ft around Rs 300 to 450, and multi-storey PEB around Rs 400 to 600. Span, height, cladding and crane load move the rate inside these bands.

    How does this PEB cost calculator work?

    You enter length, clear span and eave height. The calculator picks the rate band for your area, adjusts it for span, height, cladding, crane and city, then shows a cost range, the cost per sq ft and an indicative split by component. It is a planning estimate, not a quotation.

    Does the estimate include foundation and GST?

    No, not by default. Civil foundation and floor slab usually add Rs 150 to 350 per sq ft depending on soil and loads, and GST at 18 percent is extra. Switch both on in the calculator to see the all-in range.

    How much does a 5,000 sq ft PEB shed cost?

    A 5,000 sq ft PEB building, for example 100 ft by 50 ft with a 20 ft eave height, plain GI sheets, no crane and a Delhi location, comes to roughly Rs 12.5 lakh to Rs 16 lakh for the structure alone. Foundation and 18 percent GST are extra.

    Why does PEB cost go up with span and eave height?

    Wider clear spans need heavier, deeper steel frames. As a rule of thumb every extra 10 m of span beyond about 30 m adds roughly 8 to 12 percent. Eave height above 8 to 10 m needs taller columns and more wall cladding, so it adds cost too.

    Is PEB cheaper than a tin shed or RCC building?

    A simple tin shed is cheaper per sq ft for small areas. PEB wins for large clear spans, heavier loads and fast erection. For big factories and warehouses PEB is usually far cheaper than RCC and finishes in months instead of a year. See our comparison of industrial shed and PEB structure.
