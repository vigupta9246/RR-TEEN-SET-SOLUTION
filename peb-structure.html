PEB vs RCC

Why Choose PEB over RCC?

❌ Traditional RCC Building

  • Construction time: 6–12 months
  • Higher cost: ₹800–1200/sq.ft
  • Limited clear span: max 15–20m
  • Cannot be relocated or expanded easily
  • Heavy foundation needed

✅ PEB Structure

  • Construction time: 2–4 months
  • Lower cost: ₹220–350/sq.ft
  • Clear span up to 90m
  • Easily relocatable and expandable
  • Light foundation, saves cost

What Goes Into Your PEB Structure

  • Primary columns & rafters in IS 2062 grade structural steel

FAQ

PEB Structure Questions

PEB structure ka kharcha kitna aata hai Delhi NCR mein?

Delhi NCR mein PEB structure ₹220 se ₹420/sq.ft mein banta hai. 10,000 sq.ft ki large factory ke liye PEB ₹22–35 lakh mein complete hota hai — jo RCC se 30–40% sasta hai. Free design consultation ke liye contact karein.

PEB structure kitne time mein complete hota hai?

PEB construction RCC se 50% faster hota hai. Small PEB (up to 5000 sqft) 25–35 din mein, medium (5000–20,000 sqft) 35–55 din mein, aur large structures 50–80 din mein complete ho jaate hain.

Kya PEB structure earthquake proof hota hai?

Haan! Hamare PEB structures IS 800 aur IS 1893 (seismic) standards ke according design kiye jaate hain. Steel ki flexibility earthquake energy ko absorb karti hai — RCC se zyada safe hota hai.

PEB structure manufacturer Delhi NCR mein kaise choose karein?

PEB manufacturer choose karte waqt in cheezon ka dhyan rakhein: GST-registered company hai, design IS 800 compliant hai, material specification likhit mein di ja rahi hai, aur free site visit ke baad hi quote mil raha hai. In-house design se lekar installation tak ek hi team handle kare toh coordination behtar rehta hai.

Kya PEB structure ko apni design ke hisaab se customize kar sakte hain?

Bilkul. Span, height, roof slope, color, ventilation aur mezzanine floor — sab kuch aapki site aur requirement ke hisaab se design kiya jaata hai. Har project ka structural design plot size aur usage (factory, warehouse, ya showroom) ke according alag banta hai.

PEB structure aur prefab warehouse mein kya fark hai?

PEB ek construction method hai (pre-engineered steel framing), jabki prefab warehouse ek use-case hai jo usually PEB technology se hi banta hai. Chhote factory sheds se lekar bade warehouses tak — sab PEB principle pe based ho sakte hain, bas span aur height requirement alag hoti hai.
